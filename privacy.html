Privacy

Updated 14 October 2026. This notice covers bhavyam.info and bhavyam.shop, including the AstFPS page.

Optional website analytics

If you accept analytics, these sites use Google Analytics 4 to measure pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser details, referral sources, engagement and link or download clicks. Google processes this information to provide website reports. Analytics cookies can distinguish visits and returning browsers; they do not tell us your real-world identity.

Analytics is blocked until you accept. Advertising storage, advertising personalization and Google Signals are disabled in our tag. We do not request precise GPS location or intentionally collect names, email addresses, phone numbers or Instagram identities through analytics. Query strings and URL fragments are removed from page and custom click URLs sent by our code.

Your choice

Use “Analytics settings” at the bottom of any main page to accept or decline. Your choice is stored locally on each domain. Declining disables our analytics tag and clears its first-party analytics cookies on that domain. A browser's Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal defaults analytics to off when no prior choice exists. Declining does not remove data already collected.

Hosting and external links

GitHub Pages hosts these websites and may process connection information for delivery and security. Instagram, GitHub and Google apply their own policies when you open their links. Download-click analytics measures a click, not successful installation of a mod.
