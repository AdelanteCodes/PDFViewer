Working with your files

For tables, air waybills, searchable text and Excel exports, use the PDF Viewer. For text, images, links, drawing, signatures, forms and redaction, open the editor.

Word conversion creates a reflowed text document. PowerPoint conversion creates one page image per slide. Word/HTML to PDF uses text flow; complex layout is not preserved. Image-based tools and scan compression flatten pages and remove interactive fields, links and selectable text. Use OCR afterward when you need searchable text.
