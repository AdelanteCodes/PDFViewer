01 / RENDERING

Graphics, under the hood.

Immediate rendering optimizations and an original, opt-in terrain submission prototype. Sodium is not required.

02 / MEMORY

Make resources go further.

Memory, startup, and allocation work with bundled modules including FerriteCore, ModernFix, and Jasione.

03 / GAME LOGIC

A smoother foundation.

Lithium's game-logic optimizations, optional Chunky pregeneration, and opt-in local diagnostics.

Development snapshot: 0.4.0-dev.1. Compilation and archive checks have passed; gameplay compatibility and FPS gains remain unverified. 5× FPS is a development target, not a measured result or guarantee. Bundled modules retain their original authors and licenses.

03 / Good to know

A few answers.

Is Xe-Mod ready to download?

A development build exists, but a public download is not available here yet. This page will link to the public build once distribution is ready.

Will it give me 5× FPS?

That is the development target. It has not been measured or achieved, and results will depend on your hardware, world, settings, and other mods.

Does it change my graphics settings?

The current project does not automatically change resolution, render distance, or graphics options. The original terrain stage is experimental and opt-in.

Can I use it on a dedicated server?

The current package is a Fabric client mod for single-player and multiplayer clients. It is not a dedicated-server package.
